Privacy Policy

Survey Pocket: Land Survey

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Survey Pocket: Land Survey (“Survey Pocket”, “the app”) is a field surveying calculation and visualization application published by Fleites Tools Lab. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Location data

Survey Pocket can access your device’s foreground location when you explicitly use a feature such as “Use My Location” or add the current location as a survey point. Location is used to populate coordinates and support surveying calculations and map visualization. Survey Pocket does not use background location.

Local projects and survey data

Projects, survey points, coordinates, calculations, and related project information are stored locally on your device. Survey Pocket does not operate its own cloud account or server for uploading these projects.

Maps

The app uses Google Maps services to display map content and survey geometry. When Google Maps services are used, Google may process information according to its own privacy policy and applicable service terms.

Purchases

Survey Pocket may offer a Pro upgrade through Google Play Billing. Purchases and payment information are processed by Google Play. Survey Pocket does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

CSV export and sharing

The app can create CSV files from survey information. These files are generated from your project data and can be shared only when you choose to use Android’s sharing features. The destination you select may process the information under its own privacy practices.

Support and bug reports

The “Report a problem” feature opens your email application with a prepared support message addressed to Fleites Tools Lab. Survey Pocket does not automatically attach your coordinates, projects, personal information, or device identifiers. Any information you choose to include in an email is sent through your selected email service.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

Survey Pocket does not require a user account. The current version does not use a Fleites Tools Lab cloud backend for user projects and does not include third-party advertising or a separate analytics service operated by Fleites Tools Lab.

Third-party services

The app relies on Google services including Google Maps and Google Play Billing. Those services may collect and process data as described in Google’s privacy policies and service documentation.

Children

Survey Pocket is a professional/general-purpose surveying utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Data security and user control

Because survey projects are stored locally, you control the project information kept on your device. You can delete projects within the app. Information shared outside the app through CSV export or email is controlled by the destination service you choose.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact Fleites Tools Lab at yaselfp@yahoo.com.
